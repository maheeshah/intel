Sustainability Through the Ages

Exploring Intel’s journey toward a more sustainable future

2000 – Early Sustainability Efforts

Intel begins focusing on reducing emissions and increasing energy efficiency in manufacturing.

2010 – Renewable Energy Growth

Intel invests heavily in renewable energy and becomes one of the largest corporate users of green power.

2020 – Carbon Reduction Goals

Intel commits to aggressive carbon neutrality goals and water conservation initiatives.

2025 – Sustainable Innovation

Continued innovation in eco-friendly chip manufacturing and global sustainability leadership.
